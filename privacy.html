Privacy Policy

BDI Trainer · Bristol Dental Institute

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy explains what information BDI Trainer (the website and the iPhone and Android app) collects, why, and how you can delete it.

What we collect

  • Your account: your name and email address, used to sign you in.
  • Your progress: your training points, recent activity, the questions you got wrong and your study plan, so your progress follows you between sessions.
  • Leaderboard: your name and training points are shown to other students of the Institute on the leaderboard.
  • One device at a time: a random code that identifies your current session, so an account is used on one device at a time.

The examiner

When you answer the examiner or ask it a question, your text and the related book passages are sent to Google's Gemini service to produce the reply. Do not type personal or patient-identifying information into your answers.

Where it is stored

Account and progress data are stored with Google Firebase. Some settings are stored only on your device.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not show advertising and do not track you across other apps or websites.

Deleting your account

Open Account in the app, choose Delete my account and confirm with your password. Your account, your progress and your leaderboard entry are deleted permanently.

Education only

BDI Trainer is for education and exam preparation. It is not medical advice and does not replace clinical judgement.
